# Explorations

Every round on the way to the mark, kept as it was. Earlier rounds (an "ML" monogram and a chart-shaped logo, in orange and then in Klein) were dropped before this folder existed, when the idea switched to the signature.

| Round | What was tried | What came out of it |
|---|---|---|
| `v5-geometrica` | The handwritten "Luan" redrawn on a 45° lattice, six constructions (A–F). `construcao-esboco-luan.png` is the hand sketch over the grid. | The lattice works; the signature survives the geometry. |
| `v6-alturas` | Heights: how tall the final N stroke is relative to the start, where the A's apex sits. | The N ends as tall as the L starts; the apex sits in the middle. |
| `v7-familias` | Variations per construction (A1, A2… E3). | B1 and E1 lead. Moving the N was a dead end. |
| `v8-espelhos` | Mirrored copies of the stroke. | Mirroring hints at something bigger than one signature. |
| `v9-sigma-ezh` | Rotated 90°, the stroke reads as Σ (and its mirror as Ʒ); each refined on its own. | Σ is the hook: statistics, sum, improvement. |
| `v10-colados` | Two copies glued at different points, rotations and orientations. | A family of symbols that are "Luan" twice; 7/8 and 11 stand out. |
| `v11-ponto-simetrico` | Systematic search of offsets for a copy turned 180°, filtering out near-collisions. | Point-symmetric S candidates. |
| `v12-ajustes` | Stroke weight, gaps, and the over/under weave at the crossings. | Option 17: the woven S. |
| `v13-polimento` | Seam-free weave (masks instead of redraws), sharp vs. round corners, backgrounds, high-res. | Final: option 4 — sharp corners, straight cuts, Klein ground. |
| `v14-peso` | Optical weight compensation: light-blue copy +7%. | Imperceptible; equal weights kept. |
