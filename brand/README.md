# muanlartins — brand

![Brand board](brand-board.png)

## The mark

The mark is my signature. "Luan" is written in one continuous stroke — L, U, A, N — and redrawn on a 45° lattice so every segment is either diagonal or vertical. Turned 90°, that stroke reads as a **Σ**: the sum, the first symbol you meet in statistics, which is what this project is about — measuring how you get better.

Two copies of it, one turned 180°, are glued together and woven over and under each other at the four points where they cross. The result is a point-symmetric **S** that, under the hood, is still just "Luan" twice.

- White copy over light-blue copy at two crossings, the reverse at the other two — the alternation is what keeps the 180° symmetry.
- Sharp miter corners and straight cuts at the crossings; no rounding.
- Both copies carry the same stroke weight. An optically compensated version (light-blue copy +7%) was tested in `explorations/v14-peso/` and rejected: the difference wasn't perceptible.

The camera frame is a Klein band with its top-left and bottom-right corners cut at 45° (the mark's 180° symmetry), lined with the lattice. Two streaks, Branco and Klein claro, the mark's two copies, run around it half a lap apart. A name plate with the small mark and `muanlartins.com.br` sits on its bottom edge. Every 5 s, in turn, something moves to catch the eye: the plate's line wipes out and back in, or a sheen crosses the band at 45°, from one cut corner to the other. On the Klein ground of `conversa.html` the band is navy, to read as a frame.

The chat is Twitch's, read anonymously over IRC (`?canal=`, default `muanlartins`). It opens with the last 20 messages, from `recent-messages.robotty.de` (Twitch's IRC keeps no history), so reloading a source doesn't empty it. It shows emotes, without commands (`!…`) or bots, and with moderation deletes applied. `?demo` fills it with sample messages, to set scenes up offline.

The transition: two Klein panels close in from opposite corners along the lattice, one turned 180° from the other, each led by a streak of its copy's colour that stretches with its speed. The screen is covered while the mark draws itself, then the panels pull back into the centre line and take the mark with them.

The alerts are Twitch's own (Creator Dashboard → Alerts, "Alerts Box 1"), not a page here: Twitch knows the events, and mods can replay or skip an alert from the activity feed. Each variant runs `twitch/alertas/alerta.html` and `alerta.css` in custom HTML/CSS mode, with `{rotulo}` replaced by its label ("Novo seguidor"; "Raid · {amount} chegando"), for 7 s. It's a Klein band like the camera frame that wipes in along the diagonal while a sheen crosses it, the mark and a Klein claro label over the name. Twitch strips `@import` and scripts, so the font is an `@font-face` from Google Fonts. Subs, gifts and bits still use Twitch's default look, as the channel can't receive them before Affiliate.

To set OBS up, import the scene collection (Scene Collection → Import):

```bash
python3 brand/twitch/obs.py "C:/Users/muanl/Documents/muanlartins-obs/transicao.webm" "<Alerts Box URL>" > muanlartins.json
```

To add only what's new to the collection OBS already has (keeping its secrets), close OBS and run `python3 brand/twitch/obs.py --apply` (`--dry-run` first lists what it would add): new sources, scenes and items, transitions and unbound hotkeys, the sounds, and the Efeitos dock. It changes nothing else, but for the hotkeys it retires (`RETIRED`: F2 and F4 moved from the scenes to the dock's triggers on 2026-11-08).

The first argument is where `transicao.webm` sits on the streaming machine; the second, the Alerts Box's browser source URL (Alerts → Copy, kept out of the repo since it carries a token), adds it as "Alertas", 1100×200, centred under the scoreboard in the game scenes and under the camera in Conversa. The canvas size and the devices (camera, microphone, Rocket League's and Valorant's windows) are constants at the top of `obs.py`. Valorant's scenes ("Valorant", "Valorant + chat", "Lance ruim · Valorant") are Rocket League's with their own capture ("Captura · Valorant", which also records "… jogo.mkv"), overlays and crowd, and the camera at the top left.

The sound is split so each part has its own fader and its own track in recordings: track 1 has everything, 2 the microphone, 3 the game. The game's sound comes with its capture; Discord and the browser are captured app by app, since desktop audio would carry the game a second time. The microphone goes through NVIDIA Broadcast for noise, then a compressor and a limiter. The alerts play through OBS and the headphones. To re-render the transition after changing the page: `CHROME=/path/to/chrome node brand/twitch/transicao.mjs` (needs ffmpeg and `playwright-core`).

## Rules of thumb

- At 64px and below, use the `small/` drawing; at 16px, the favicon.
- Keep the mark on Klein or on a transparent background over Klein. On anything else, it needs the Klein ground behind it.
- Don't recolor the copies, round the corners, or rotate the mark — the 180° symmetry is the point.

## Regenerating

`build.py` is the source of truth: every file above, and the website's icons (`app/icon.svg`, `app/favicon.ico`, `app/apple-icon.png`, `app/opengraph-image.png`, `public/brand/`), comes out of it.

```bash
cd brand
python3 -m venv .venv && ./.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt   # once
./.venv/bin/python build.py
```

Needs `rsvg-convert` (librsvg) and ImageMagick (`magick`): `brew install librsvg imagemagick`.
