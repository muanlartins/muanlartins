# ~20-minute "ranked with friends" videos cut from a live: research for the script

Researched 2026-11-17. Format: Rocket League 3v3 ranked with friends on Discord. Each video holds 2–3 whole matches from
one live, and the full VOD stays back for ~3 days. Shorts editing is out of scope; its edge rules are referenced, not
repeated, from `muanlartins-shorts/.claude/skills/estudio/pesquisa-cortes.md` (rules I2–I7, T1–T2).

**Tags** (as in the shorts doc, plus one): **[DATA]** measured · **[PLATFORM]** YouTube or Epic official ·
**[PRO]** working creator or editor · **[VENDOR]** tool blog · **[SYNTH]** my conclusion · **[OBS]** observed on real
channels (titles, durations, views and chapters pulled with yt-dlp on 2026-11-17).

**Where the evidence stands.** Thin: no study measures cold-open length, intro length or cut density for gaming
long-form. Strongest: YouTube's help pages and July 2026 Short→video guide, Todd Beaupré's Sept 2026 Creator Insider
interview, one observational series study (OverseerOS), and RL creators who ship exactly this format. Effects and
pacing are craft opinion; Luan's own verdict (no chopped edits or tacky effects, gameplay whole) is the best evidence
about *his* audience, and it matches the observed RL format.

## 1. Structure

### 1a. Cold open
- **[PLATFORM]** YouTube's 2026 Related-video guide: the long video should "open with the promised topic within the
  first 5 to 10 seconds, skipping slow intros, logos, and unrelated updates"
  ([blog.youtube](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-related-videos-traffic-guide/)), which
  matters because his Shorts will link here (§4).
- **[PLATFORM]** Studio's "Intro" figure is the share still watching at 30 s; for a low figure, YouTube's advice is to
  change the first 30 s ([Help 9314415](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415)).
- **[PLATFORM, 2011]** Grab attention first and tease the rest; "a flashy intro… is not the star"
  ([Creator Playbook](https://blog.youtube/creator-and-artist-stories/youtube-creator-playbook-tips-first-15)).
  **[PRO, other genre]** "Stop telling people what they will be watching and start showing them" (leaked MrBeast
  guide, [memo](https://www.alexanderjarvis.com/memo-how-to-succeed-in-mrbeast-production/)).
- **[VENDOR]** Teasers of 3–15 s are quoted, and a teaser "should not spoil the payoff"
  ([Creator Essentials](https://www.creatoressentials.com/glossary/cold-open/)). Nothing measured.
- **[SYNTH] One moment, not a montage.** A 3–4 moment montage at ~3 s each is exactly the chopped feel Luan rejected,
  and the shorts research found that many setups in a short span confuse (its S1–S3). Use **one moment, 8–15 s,
  played whole** with its reaction, both edges on mic silence. Take it from **match 2 or 3 of the same video**: the
  payoff then comes back in context later, and the title and thumbnail promise exactly that moment.

### 1b. Luan's recorded intro (15–20 s, camera talking about the channel)
- **[PLATFORM]** The reused-content policy lists "same intro and outro for your videos, but the bulk of your content is
  different" as allowed ([Help 1311392](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392)).
- **[VENDOR]** Self-introductions in the first 30 s "consistently cause retention drops"; do it "briefly after the
  hook", with a brand sting of 3 s or less. Nothing measured.
- **[SYNTH]** Put it right after the cold open, as planned, and cap cold open + intro at **40 s**, so the first kickoff
  is on screen by 0:40. A 20 s pitch is above every quoted range, so its position is a test (rule R22).

### 1c. Downtime between matches (queue, menus, lobby talk)
- **[VENDOR]** Cut loading, queue and menus, then "put back comedic beats and tension beats"
  ([Cutsio](https://cutsio.com/blog/turn-long-streams-into-youtube-videos)).
- **[PRO]** Mario Joos, a retention editor: slow pacing "comes from stacking together too many non-progressive
  scenes", not from cut speed ([Vidpros](https://vidpros.com/insiders/mario-joos-youtube-retention-secrets/)).
- **[OBS]** SpookyLuke's chapters leave no room for gaps; Squishy bridges with one line ("All right, for the first game…").
- **[SYNTH]** Keep banter only when it is speech about the game or the friends: whole sentences, at most 15 s per gap;
  otherwise cut straight to the next kickoff countdown. No full-screen title cards; chapters do the navigation. An
  optional "Partida 2" lower-third over the first countdown (2–3 s) is the most to add; no source for or against it.

### 1d. Inside a match: replays, overtime, the post-match screen
- **Goal replays: no source measured this.** Players routinely skip replays in-game
  ([PC Gamer](https://pcgamer.com/five-annoying-things-that-rocket-league-players-still-do-years-later)), and RL
  clipping tools cut the replay and the kickoff routine [VENDOR]. **[SYNTH]** Cut the replay unless people are talking
  over it or it's the goal the video is sold on. Re-enter at the kickoff countdown and keep its full 3 s, a natural
  "here we go".
- **Overtime: always whole [SYNTH].** It's the climax (the MrBeast guide's build-to-the-payoff logic). If overtime
  pushes three matches past ~23 min, use two matches.
- **Post-match: 5–12 s [SYNTH].** Result screen, reaction, and a rank or division change if it shows. Cut at the first
  mic silence once the result is clear.
- **Live play is never cut**, kickoff to goal. Take kickoff, goal and end times from the RL replays the studio already
  syncs to `Live.mp4`, not from image detection.

### 1e. Ending
- **[PLATFORM]** The end screen occupies the last 5–20 s of a video at least 25 s long and holds up to 4 elements;
  "leave enough space and time at the end of the video" ([Help 6388789](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6388789)).
  A "specific video" element can only point to a public or unlisted video, so a scheduled VOD can't be targeted yet.
- **[PRO]** "Don't ever signal the end… endings must always be abrupt to protect retention" (MrBeast guide).
  **[SYNTH]** Final post-match, then the ~20 s outro with the end-screen elements on from its first frame: one line
  pointing at the next video, not a goodbye.

## 2. Pacing and graphics
- **Cut density.** No gaming long-form data exists. **[OBS]** The ranked-session format is one continuous take per
  game. **[PRO]** vidIQ's host used to aim for "a visual change every 4 to 7 seconds". He now calls that style overused,
  says "retention editing often comes at the cost of connection", and wants calm content edited calmly
  ([vidIQ](https://rosetta.to/u/vidiq/retention-editing-is-dead)). Joos: "If you are looking at fast cuts, then you are
  looking in the wrong place." **[SYNTH]** Cut only at dead time. With 3 matches of ~5 goals each, expect roughly 15–25
  cuts per 20 min.
- **Commentary flow.** No edge may fall inside speech. Apply the shorts doc's I2–I7: snap to silence, keep the breath,
  micro-fade every edge, J/L-cuts only across non-speech audio.
- **Zooms, SFX, memes, music: none [SYNTH].** The vidIQ material and Luan's own rejection agree; RL already has crowd
  noise, goal explosions and his crowd overlay.
- **Captions and counters.** No long-form gaming source covers either. **[SYNTH]** No burned-in captions (a Shorts
  convention): upload a pt-BR CC track from the transcript, re-timed through the edit list (youtube.py already does
  this for VODs). RL's HUD shows score and clock and his scene shows the rank plate, so add nothing persistent.

## 3. Titles and thumbnails
- **[DATA, observational]** OverseerOS compared 520 series videos from 115 channels with the same channels' standalone
  uploads ([OverseerOS](https://www.overseeros.com/blog/do-youtube-series-get-more-views)). The median series video
  reached **0.92×** the standalone baseline (gaming also 0.92×). In 35 matched pairs, **Episode 2 got a median 61%** of
  Episode 1's views and only 17% beat it; Episode 3 got 76% of Episode 2. Where the number sits in the title was not
  tested.
- **[PRO, anecdotal]** vidIQ: "don't put episode numbers in your thumbnails". If numbers are needed, put them at the end
  of the title, and make each video stand alone ([vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/stop-using-episode-numbers-gaming-content)).
- **[PLATFORM]** Beaupré: viewers "don't want to be picking up the remote every five to 10 minutes", which is why
  series hold ([PPC Land, Sep 2026](https://ppc.land/subscribers-skip-90-of-uploads-in-their-feed-youtube-director-says/)).
  Chain the videos with the playlist and end screen, not with "Parte 1/2/3".
- **[OBS]** Hook first (caps, a feeling or a question), series or game tag last: "<hook> | Road to SSL #22";
  "PEGUEI PLATINA! - Rumo ao Grand Champion 1v1 #4 | Rocket League". Brazilian friend content uses "COM OS AMIGOS" or
  "(ft. nome)".
- **[SYNTH]** Three standalone titles, each from its own cold-open moment, no "Parte N"; optional tail "| Rocket League"
  or "com os amigos". Thumbnail = that moment's frame plus his face (thumb.py sheet → Luan picks; his judgment call).
  No RL-specific thumbnail evidence found; the generic advice (face, one subject, consistency) is [VENDOR].

## 4. Publishing
- **Highlights before the VOD.** No source names this exact sequence. **[PRO]** The
  [SSW rule](https://www.ssw.com.au/rules/manage-youtube-livestream-content/) keeps raw stream and edit as separate,
  cross-linked videos; **[OBS]** alanzoka keeps full lives on a separate channel. **[PLATFORM]** "The system evaluates
  each piece of content individually"; "Shorts, VODs, or livestreams… will not inherently confuse the algorithm"
  ([Help 16559651](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16559651)), though long-term performance can suffer if
  viewers keep ignoring recommended videos. **[SYNTH]** The later VOD doesn't hurt the edits, and at 0–3 views there's
  nothing to cannibalise; the hold-back keeps the worse-packaged copy out of search and suggested while the edits are
  new. Keep it.
- **Spacing.**
  - **[PLATFORM]** Viewers get at most **3 upload/live notifications per channel per 24 h**; you can untick "notify
    subscribers" on extras ([Help 7457584](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7457584)).
  - **[PLATFORM]** Waiting before publishing gives no benefit, and scheduling is fine. Rene Ritchie: "Waiting 24 to 48
    hours is no different than waiting 24 to 48 seconds"
    ([SEJ, Nov 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-debunks-24-48-hour-upload-delay-recommendation/560613/)).
    Posting in "off hours" may slow the first boost (Ritchie on X, reported second-hand).
  - **[DATA, correlational]** Frequency isn't penalised; per-video views may fall as volume rises while channel totals
    grow (vidIQ's 10 M-channel study, OverseerOS).
  - **[SYNTH]** Lives are near-daily (11-09 to 11-13, 11-17). Publishing one video per live per day means 3 long
    videos a day in steady state, each from a different live: variety, and two videos from the same session never
    compete on the same day.
- **Time of day for Brazil.** No Brazil-specific data found; vendor windows (15–17 h local for long-form) are generic.
  **[PLATFORM]** Studio → Analytics → Audience → "When your viewers are on YouTube" (28 days); use it once it has data.
  Until then, avoid 00–09 h BRT.
- **Chapters [PLATFORM].** First timestamp 00:00, at least 3 in ascending order, each at least 10 s
  ([Help 9884579](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9884579)). Neutral names, as SpookyLuke uses ("Game 1"), so
  no score spoilers.
- **End screens and playlists.** *(Luan, 2026-11-28: no end screens on any video, he won't set them up; the
  playlists still hold.)* Set end screens in Studio: the Data API has no end-screen endpoint (no doc found, only
  unanswered requests). Use "Best for viewer", the series playlist and Subscribe; all stay valid without later edits.
  Create one playlist for the edits, separate from "Rocket League ao vivo".
- **Shorts → long.** **[PLATFORM]** In Studio, a Short's "Related video" makes a clickable link to any video on the
  channel. YouTube's tips: a CTA in the Short's last 5 s, and a long video that pays off the click within 5–10 s. Only
  Studio can set it; publishing APIs can't [VENDOR]. His Shorts already reach 50–1,300 views, so this is the cheapest
  traffic the edits will get.

## 5. Policy risk
- **Reused content: low risk.**
  - **[PLATFORM]** Allowed: "content that primarily features the uploading creator", and the same intro and outro
    across videos. YouTube's own Studio tool turns your live into "a shorter, edited version"
    ([Help 4522163](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/4522163)).
  - **[PLATFORM]** Not allowed: other people's clips "with little or no narrative", and "inauthentic" content, i.e.
    "similar or repetitive content with low… commentary… or minimal variation across videos" or "templated
    storylines". The policy is judged channel-wide.
  - **[SYNTH]** The residual risk is volume: ~15 templated videos a week with an identical intro could look
    mass-produced at a YPP review. Mitigate it: his voice runs throughout, no match appears in two edits, and titles
    and thumbnails differ. The spam policy also allows "a few variations of a video"
    ([Help 2801973](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973)).
- **Epic / Psyonix [PLATFORM].** The [Fan Content Policy](https://legal.epicgames.com/epicgames/fan-art-policy) covers
  any Epic game whose EULA you accepted. **1.4** "individual fans are permitted to monetize web videos (such as YouTube)
  with advertisements". **1.5** Epic can revoke this at any time. **1.10** Required disclaimer: *"Portions of the
  materials used are trademarks and/or copyrighted works of Epic Games, Inc. All rights reserved by Epic. This material
  is not official and is not endorsed by Epic."* **1.11** "Rocket League" may be used only to describe the content.
- **Music.**
  - Epic can only license its own IP; licensed tracks (Monstercat, Player Anthems) belong to others. Monstercat says it
    claims videos using its music without a Gold licence (second-hand; its page has moved).
  - **[PLATFORM]** With **Streamer Safe Music** on (Settings → Stream Safe tab), RL "only plays Music and Player Anthems
    that do not breach DMCA" ([Epic help](https://www.epicgames.com/help/rocket-league-c-202300000001622/a202300000015836)),
    a feature since Season 4, 2021 ([GGRecon](https://www.ggrecon.com/articles/rocket-league-copyright-free-music/)).
  - **[PLATFORM]** Content ID claims usually monetise or track the video and "usually don't impact your channel"
    ([Help 6013276](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6013276)).
  - **Ask Luan:** is Streamer Safe on? A claim on a VOD reappears on every edit cut from it. Check non-game audio too
    (Pix songs, stingers).

## Rules for the script

Times are on the `Live.mp4` clock; match, kickoff and goal times come from the synced replays.

**Selection**
- **R1.** 3 videos per RL live (A, B, C). Each holds whole ranked matches only, in chronological order inside the
  video; a match appears in at most one video.
- **R2.** Use 3 matches if they total 23 min or less after R7–R11, otherwise 2. Target 17–23 min; hard maximum 25 min.
  If even 2 matches exceed 25 min (long overtime), use 1.
- **R3.** Spread the hooks: rank the live's moments and give the top 3 to different videos. A takes the strongest.

**Order inside a video (fixed)**
- **R4.** Cold open → intro → match 1 → … → match N → outro. The first kickoff is on screen by **0:40 at the latest**.
- **R5.** Cold open: exactly **1 moment, 8–15 s**, from match 2 or 3 of the same video. It plays whole and ends on the
  reaction; both edges on mic silence (shorts I2–I5); no text, music or SFX. Hard cut into the intro.
- **R6.** Intro: the recorded 15–20 s clip, unaltered. Hard cut to match 1 at the kickoff countdown start.

**Inside a match**
- **R7.** No cut between a kickoff and the next goal or the final whistle.
- **R8.** After a goal: keep the goal plus 3 s, cut the replay, re-enter at the start of the kickoff countdown (keep
  its full 3 s). Keep the replay only if mic speech overlaps it (until that speech ends at silence) or it is the
  video's cold-open goal.
- **R9.** Overtime is always kept in full.
- **R10.** Post-match: **5–12 s** from the final whistle or winning goal through the result screen, keeping a rank or
  division animation if present. End at the first mic silence after the result shows.

**Between matches**
- **R11.** Cut from the R10 end to the next kickoff countdown. Exception: banter, meaning whole utterances only,
  **15 s or less per gap**, and only when speech is present. Otherwise the gap is 0 s.
- **R12.** Every edge snaps to mic silence and gets a micro-fade (shorts I3–I6). Hard cuts only; no designed
  transitions.

**Graphics and audio**
- **R13.** 0 zooms, 0 SFX, 0 added music, 0 burned-in captions, no persistent counters.
- **R14.** Optional, off by default: a lower-third "Partida N" for 2.5 s over each match's first countdown.
- **R15.** Captions: a pt-BR SRT from the live transcript, re-timed through the edit list, uploaded as a CC track.

**Ending and metadata**
- **R16.** The outro is last; the end screen covers the **last 20 s** (allowed 5–20 s). Elements: Best for viewer, the
  series playlist, Subscribe. Set in Studio by hand.
- **R17.** Chapters computed from the edit list: "00:00 Destaques", "Partida 1", "Partida 2", ("Partida 3"), "Final".
  At least 3 chapters, each at least 10 s; names never include scores.
- **R18.** Titles (Luan picks): hook first, from that video's cold-open moment. No "Parte N", no number in the
  thumbnail; optional tail "| Rocket League" or "com os amigos".
- **R19.** Description: line 1 the hook; the teammates (aliados); links to the other two videos and to the live VOD
  once public; the Epic disclaimer verbatim (§5).

**Publishing**
- **R20.** For a live on day N: A on N+1, B on N+2, C on N+3, the VOD public on N+4 (at least 24 h after C). Never 2
  long videos from the same live on one day; at most 3 long uploads per day in total. Default slots 12:00, 16:00 and
  19:00 BRT, replaced once Studio's "When your viewers are on YouTube" has 28 days of data. Never
  publish 00:00–09:00 BRT.
- **R21.** Upload early; schedule only after Studio's copyright check passes. Add each video to the series playlist,
  set "Related video" on each Short from that live to the long video containing its moment, and link the VOD's
  description to A, B and C.
- **R22.** Review after 9 videos (3 lives): compare Studio's Intro (% at 30 s) and Key moments. If retention falls
  faster across the intro than across the cold open, move the intro to the first gap (between matches 1 and 2). If it
  still dips, cut it to its first 8–10 s.
